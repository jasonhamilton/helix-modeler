# Fixtures

- `golden/`: the two golden `.hlx` templates, one exported from Helix Native in HX Stomp compatibility mode and one from HX Edit with the pedal attached. They are the user's own exports. They are **gitignored by default**; whether to commit them is the human's call (open question in `docs/PLAN.md`, Legal). Every generated preset is diffed against them by `hlx-validate`.
- `smoke/`: `di_2s.wav`, a 2 s clean DI clip from a licence-clean source (EGDB, or a clip the human records), used by `engine-smoke`. `known.hlx` is a tiny known-good preset generated from a golden template with one amp, one cab and a bypassed delay.
- `synthetic/`: generated by `synthetic-benchmark`; large, gitignored, regenerated from its manifest.
