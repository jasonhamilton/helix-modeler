# Methodology audit, 2026-10-23 (independent, read-only)

Scope: the primary records only (docs/DECISIONS.md, ledger/runs.csv at 2,286 rows, board/*.md, docs/notes/*.md, reports/status/README.md, local cache/matches/ listings). Chat summaries were not used. Nothing was run. Citations are `file:line` or ledger `timestamp / dataset`. Where a number comes from an unadmitted metric it is marked **[unadmitted]**.

## 0. The five findings that matter most

1. **No progress claim since 2026-10-14 rests on a blind test with a sealed set and a pre-registered bar.** The one gate that was run that way (Gate 2, three sealed sets) was afterwards judged "unusable" by the human (DECISIONS.md:1867-1880) and is still unsigned (board/GATES.md Gate 2). Gate 3 has no sealed set and no bars (board/TASKS.md:160 [T159](../../board/TASKS.md#t159 "Gate 3 blind single-block set") open). Every claim after 10-18 is either a numeric result on an unsealed set built by the same agent that built the method, or an open (unblinded) listen by one listener.
2. **AFx-Rep distance is validated against the ear only for ordering *models* on the *same performance*; it is now the objective for knob fine-tuning and for "closer" claims across *different dry tracks*, where the project's own measurement says it does not work** ([T175](../../board/TASKS.md#t175 "Different-guitar test of embed_afxrep, ltas_eqfit, ltas_tilt on drive"): invariance ratio 30 for small knob moves, "no score sets drive knobs across DIs", DECISIONS.md:2065-2068). The human's 10-18 ruling said knobs are not set by a sound-comparison score (DECISIONS.md:2167); [T209](../../board/TASKS.md#t209 "Knob fine-tuning on the seven T207 picks") and the new `song_match.py` default do exactly that (scripts/song_match.py:41-43).
3. **The human's own test-retest consistency on repeated blind pairs is 7 of 12** (v2 1/2, v3 1/3, v4 1/2, v5 2/2, v6 1/2, v7 1/1; ledger `retest_consistent` rows 2026-10-18T20:25 to 2026-10-20T22:44). Sessions of 6 to 25 decided trials therefore cannot resolve the differences they are read as resolving (v6: "c beat the tutorial" on 6 decided trials with 1 of 2 repeats consistent, DECISIONS.md:2677-2693).
4. **The approval rule of 10-18 is followed in form and bypassed in substance.** Approvals since then are one-word answers to bundled lists: "go ahead" covering 12 items (DECISIONS.md:2008-2026, 2160-2171), "yes do those" (2537), "yes" covering 13 slot-dataset design decisions (2860-2864), "you have 12 hours on the cpu. run what you need" read as approval of two data-fix options (2901-2903). Meanwhile two methods became de facto defaults with no approval entry: AFx-Rep final ranking (listed as "blocked on the human" in board/CYCLES.md, then used in [T200](../../board/TASKS.md#t200 "Blind single-drive matches of five songs the human added to data/songs"), [T207](../../board/TASKS.md#t207 "All seven songs (human 2026-10-22 \"render the dry tracks for all of…"), [T211](../../board/TASKS.md#t211 "All seven songs with the calibrated guitar") and baked into `song_match.py`) and knob fine-tuning by AFx-Rep distance with Output/Master free.
5. **Effort went in circles twice and is about to a third time.** Gate 2 (10-16 to 10-18, three sealed sets, specialists, a level term) was declared unusable the day it passed. The distortion-fingerprint route (10-18, [T171](../../board/TASKS.md#t171 "Distortion identification steps 0 and 1")-[T181](../../board/TASKS.md#t181 "Step 4: identify model and settings from real guitar takes at random…"), 1,200+ Native renders) produced an output-only identifier at 0.06-0.08 top-1 and a DI-known inversion no better than the coarse grid (DECISIONS.md:2296-2314), and was proposed for stopping the same day. 240,000 slot renders ([T213](../../board/TASKS.md#t213 "slot-dataset-v1 overnight run")) were made before anyone checked whether single-block heads survive a chain; the next day [T214](../../board/TASKS.md#t214 "Chain transfer test (human 2026-10-23 \"yes\")") showed they do not (drive 0.02, amp 0.01 top-1; DECISIONS.md:2932-2939). The gap was already named in [T202](../../board/TASKS.md#t202 "Training-data coverage report per slot") ("no chains") before the render.

## 3. Test-design history and the approval rule

**Gate 2 (10-16 to 10-18).** Three sealed sets consumed (v1, v2, v3; GATES.md "All three sets are SPENT"). Between attempts the admitted score changed (level_db, DECISIONS.md:1569), the tie rule changed (extended rule B, human, 1476), a family prior was adopted on a 60-item dev set with one run per arm (1600), and a mod/drive path was rejected after its tuning-half gain did not transfer (1451-1464). The retrospective admits "across attempts that is mild tuning to the gate" (1689). Bars: model 0.60 against a random walk at 0.56-0.60; the non-inferiority check allows being 0.05 *worse* than random (890-899). The confirmation run on an untouched v4 ([T152](../../board/TASKS.md#t152 "Gate 2 confirmation run on a fresh sealed synthetic-v4 with the…")) and the harness sanity checks ([T155](../../board/TASKS.md#t155 "Test-validity checks")) are still open (TASKS.md:155,158). Gate 2 remains unsigned and the human has said the model is unusable.

**Gate 3.** Defined 10-18 with the rule that `benchmark` registers bars before the set is generated (1726-1739). Five days later: no bars, no set, [T159](../../board/TASKS.md#t159 "Gate 3 blind single-block set") open. The predictor-first and no-DI results that stand in for it were produced by `search` and `learning` on sets they drew themselves.

## 7. Ten methodology recommendations

1. **Stop making "closer" claims from distances across different dry tracks.** Any comparison of two runs that differ in the dry signal must be judged blind by ear or not at all. Replace the T211/T207/chord numbers in the status report with "no ear verdict".
2. **Run the missing ablation on AFx-Rep against a separated stem.** Take 5 songs x 5 candidates (the [T207](../../board/TASKS.md#t207 "All seven songs (human 2026-10-22 \"render the dry tracks for all of…") top-5), render each on the same rebuilt dry, and have the human rank them blind. Report Spearman between AFx distance and the ranks. Until this exists, AFx-Rep is unvalidated for the only target the product has.
3. **Ablate fine-tuning properly.** Same model, same dry: [T207](../../board/TASKS.md#t207 "All seven songs (human 2026-10-22 \"render the dry tracks for all of…") knobs vs [T209](../../board/TASKS.md#t209 "Knob fine-tuning on the seven T207 picks") knobs vs [T209](../../board/TASKS.md#t209 "Knob fine-tuning on the seven T207 picks") knobs with Output/Master locked, blind, sides randomised, each pair repeated once. n >= 14 pairs (7 songs x 2). The current "same or better" is one open pass.
4. **Ablate the calibration profile one factor at a time** (level only; tone only; own notes only), as state-of-project-2026-10-23.md already asks; otherwise [T204](../../board/TASKS.md#t204 "Guitar calibration (human 2026-10-22)") cannot be credited.
5. **Pre-register and seal Gate 3 now, with P3 as the reserve and bars set by `benchmark`, before the chain pilot's results arrive.** Every predictor number so far is on sets drawn by `search` or `learning`.
6. **Measure the listener before measuring the methods.** Every session should carry at least 6 repeats; report the human's own consistency first; treat differences smaller than the repeat noise as ties. At 7 of 12 consistency, a 6-trial session (v6) has no power.
7. **Sample sizes.** For a 0.15 difference in a paired proportion at 80% power you need about 50 discordant-capable pairs; for the ear, 30+ decided trials per question. v2-v6 range 6-25. Either pool questions across sessions with a pre-stated analysis or accept "directional" and say so in the dashboard.
8. **Separate "proposal", "test setting" and "default" in code.** A flag used as a test setting in one task must not become the hard-coded default in a packaging task without a DECISIONS entry quoting the human (song_match.py:41-43, 450, 453 and shortlist.py families default are the current violations). Add a test that asserts `song_match.py` method flags match a `data/approved-methods.json` the lead may only change with a human quote.
9. **Replace bundled approvals with itemised ones.** A "yes" to a list of 13 is recorded as 13 approvals; require one line per item with the human's words, or record "bundle approval" and treat each item as provisional.
10. **Stop or defer:** new scoring methods (the lead already proposes this); more open listens of drive-only matches; rebuild-alignment tuning on an uninformative metric; further single-block slot rendering until the [T215](../../board/TASKS.md#t215 "Chain training pilot") chain pilot reports; status-report growth (2,211 lines) that restates unadmitted numbers as improvements. Resume the Gate 2 harness sanity check ([T155](../../board/TASKS.md#t155 "Test-validity checks")) and a v4 confirmation ([T152](../../board/TASKS.md#t152 "Gate 2 confirmation run on a fresh sealed synthetic-v4 with the…")) only if Gate 2 is still wanted; otherwise close it formally so it stops being reported as "3 of 3 evidenced".
