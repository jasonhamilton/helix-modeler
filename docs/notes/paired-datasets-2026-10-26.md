## 1. The answer in one paragraph

Yes, several exist, and the lead should know three things. (1) **None is rendered through Helix or HX**: the wet side is real pedals/amps (EGFxSet, ToneTwist, Amp-Space, Guitar-TECHS mic), NAM/Proteus captures (GOAT, Open-Amp) or other vendors' plug-ins (GUITAR-FX-DIST, EGDB-PG, IDMT). So no public set gives us (c) for Helix model identity; our own renders stay the training source. (2) The real-hardware sets with **labelled knobs** (EGFxSet, ToneTwist parametric subsets, Amp-Space) are the first chance to test the known-DI matcher against a physical pedal the Helix models (TS808, RAT, Big Muff, Marshall JVM, Mesa): render the dataset's own DI through the Helix counterpart, search, and see whether the found knobs track the labelled knobs. That is use (b) and needs no new recordings. (3) The licence chain is the trap: the IDMT datasets are CC BY-NC-ND, and ToneTwist, GUITAR-FX-DIST and Open-Amp all take their dry audio from IDMT, so anything released that was trained on them inherits an NC question (flags in section 4).

**Capture communities.** TONE3000 (ex-ToneHunt), Kemper Rig Exchange and TONEX ToneNET distribute **models/profiles, not DI/reamp audio**. TONE3000's T3K licence lets you use a model's outputs freely but not redistribute the file; uploads grant TONE3000 an internal-research licence (FACT, [about](https://www.tone3000.com/about) and search snippet of the licence page). Kemper and IK terms for profiles were not found this session (UNVERIFIED). So the only route is "use captures as a renderer" as GOAT and Open-Amp did; the per-profile licence is each uploader's and would need checking per profile. Not a dataset; (d) as data, (c) recipe at best. NAM itself is MIT (FACT); its training signal's licence not stated on the repo page.

**Bass.** Covered only by IDMT-SMT-Audio-Effects (NC-ND), the ToneTwist dry set and Martínez Ramírez; no bass-amp set with labelled settings was found (UNVERIFIED that none exists).

## 3. What to do with them (proposals; the human decides, CLAUDE.md standing rule)

1. **Real-pedal check of the known-DI drive matcher** on EGFxSet (CC BY, clean). Take the clean note for a pickup config, the RAT2 and Mini TS wet files and their knob rows; run the existing drive search on the Helix counterparts (Vermin Dist, Scream 808, per `docs/notes/distortion-circuits.md`); report whether the found Gain/Tone track the labelled knobs and what the AFx-Rep distance is against a real pedal rather than a Native render. Cost: a few hundred renders, 5.8 GB download, no training. First public evidence of Helix-vs-real transfer; no approval needed beyond the download (measurement), but the download should be recorded in `data/di/README.md` with the attribution line.
2. **Same on ToneTwist's parametric Marshall JVM410H and Big Muff** once the wet records' licences are read (expect CC BY-NC like the dry; fine for a private measurement, not for shipped weights).
3. **Ask Naradowsky for the RockingFace licence** before any download; it is the only public set with knob values across 50 devices.
4. Do **not** fold IDMT-derived audio (IDMT, GUITAR-FX-DIST, ToneTwist dry, Open-Amp) into any training set for released weights.
5. GOAT's DI is the best riff-like DI we could add to the library, but "research only, by request" conflicts with a released model trained on it; use it, if at all, for evaluation only, and record the request terms.
